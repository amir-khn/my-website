About Us

TravelBloom is a travel recommendation website created to help travelers explore inspiring beaches, historic temples, and memorable countries around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make discovering and comparing travel destinations clear, visual, and effortless for everyday travelers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted, easy-to-use destination guide that inspires thoughtful and culturally respectful travel worldwide.

Our Core Values

  • Clarity & Honesty: Share accurate, helpful destination details without clutter.
  • Cultural Respect: Highlight local heritage, landmarks, and traditions thoughtfully.
  • Continuous Improvement: Keep refining content, design, and user experience together.

Guidance & Instructions for Team Members

Team members contributing to this project should follow these guidelines:

  • Use Consistent File Names: Keep filenames lowercase (index.html, about.html, contact.html, and style.css) in the root folder.
  • Check Links & Images: Confirm that navigation links work across pages and every destination card includes a working image URL and descriptive alt text.
  • Test Features Before Submitting: Test the Search and Clear buttons with keywords like beach, temple, and country, and verify the live GitHub Pages site.
  • Document Changes Clearly: Write clear commit messages when updating files in GitHub.
